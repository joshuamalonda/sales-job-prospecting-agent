My approach

Prospecting strategy · 24-day cadence

Create familiarity before the call, then keep the account warm across multiple channels and touchpoints.

  1. 1
    Day 1First touch

    Personalised email, LinkedIn connection note and a phone call.

    No pickup: leave a voicemail referencing the email.

  2. 2
    Day 3–5Add a personal touch

    Automated email, plus a Loom video or a voice note on LinkedIn.

    Connection request still not accepted: engage with their posts and use InMail.

  3. 3
    Day 9–12Call and follow up

    Cold call, follow-up email and a LinkedIn follow-up.

    No pickup: leave a voice note referencing the pain.

  4. 4
    Day 13–21Keep the account warm

    More cold calls and cold emails, following the cadence steps above.

  5. 5
    Day 24Close the loop

    Final email offering to close the loop, then move the account into an email nurture sequence.

    Revisit the account in a couple of months.
